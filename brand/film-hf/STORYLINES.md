# Weave brand film: three storylines

The one thing: you can see how it was said, not only what was said.
What the viewer keeps: "the word changed shape with the voice."
The spine: one word, Weave, said different ways. The hero word is the main part in all three.

Assumptions: 30 to 40 s, 1920 by 1080 at 30 fps, no voiceover. Silent unless a music track is supplied; the catalog needs a HeyGen sign-in, which is not used. Nothing has to be a real page, but the type is the real hero face and the caption rules are Weave's own.

## 1. Say it three ways (recommended)

Device: the feature as the hook. Form: one frame that rebuilds.

The word Weave fills the frame and is said three times. Whispered, it stands thin and tall. Spoken, it settles. Shouted, it drops heavy and wide and the ground floods with a speaker's colour. Then one plain sentence gets the same treatment, so the viewer sees the word was a caption all along.

- First two seconds: the word, thin and tall, white on black, already narrowing as a small "whispered" sits under it.
- Beats: whisper → spoken → shout on a colour field → a second voice answers in a second colour → one sentence said three ways → claim → the word settles as the mark.
- Look: black and white from the hero page, Roboto Flex on its weight and width axes, speaker colours as whole fields.
- Sound: near silence for the whisper, a pulse enters on "spoken", the shout lands on the first strong beat.

## 2. Inside the word (the bold one)

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom.

The camera starts inside the W and never cuts. It dives into a stroke and finds a caption line, into one word and finds its letters lifting in syllables, into one letter and finds the wave that drives it, then pulls all the way back until the whole word moves with that wave.

- First two seconds: one white stroke of the W edge to edge, thickening, the camera drifting in.
- Beats: inside the W → the line → the word → the syllable → the wave → pull back, the word is the wave → end card.
- Look: black and white until the wave arrives in speaker yellow; colour only after it.
- Sound: one tone that rises with each dive and resolves on the pull back.

## 3. Volume

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.

Giant words set in the hero's type, one per cut, each on a speaker colour field: whisper in hairline, talk in regular, SHOUT at full weight and width, then fast, slow, who. Each word looks like how it sounds. The last cut is Weave.

- First two seconds: the word "whisper" at hairline weight, tiny in a yellow field, then the cut.
- Beats: whisper → talk → SHOUT → fast and slow → two colours for two voices → claim → Weave.
- Look: full colour fields from the speaker palette, black type, no black ground until the mark.
- Sound: a dry drum pattern; every cut is a hit, the shout is the loudest.
